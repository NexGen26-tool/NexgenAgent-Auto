# Time per video (measured Oct 19-22)

| Run | Videos | Elapsed | Hands-on | Per video |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One product alone | 1 | 19 min | ~8 min | 19 min |
| Two products side by side | 2 | 19 min | ~10 min | ~10 min |
| 8 variant batches from existing storyboards | 8 | ~10 min to launch all | ~1-2 min each | waits on Grok |
| CapCut house edit on 3 timelines + VO | 3 | seconds by script | ~2 min | under 1 min |

One video alone: about 19 minutes from empty fields to edited timeline (storyboard ~4.5 min, brief ~3 min written
while waiting, video batch ~6 min, edit ~2 min).

A week of 12 in one sitting, no outages (estimate): storyboards ~7 min, briefs ~15 min while they run, 12 video
batches = 48 Grok parts at ~79 s with 2 at a time ~32 min, export and CapCut ~5 min. About 1 hour, so about
5 minutes a video. The human check of each timeline comes on top.

Batching does not beat the Grok queue (2 parts at a time) or outages, which cost far more than any step.

## Week of Oct 26 (measured Oct 28)

The 9 final batches (3 groups) took about 40 minutes from first fill to last download (Grok queue), the shot check
about 20 minutes, the edit plan and build about 30. What made the week take a day was not these steps but four route
changes before them (see `lessons.md`, "What cost the day"). Run as written, a week of 12 is about 3-4 hours.
