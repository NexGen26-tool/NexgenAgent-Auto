# Camera rule

The camera rule lives in the ad-brief-compiler skill (section 2, "Camera: moving, but the room stays readable"):
one real camera move per scene row, most rows eye-level medium or wide with the person in the lived-in room, at
most two close details, no crash zooms, whip pans, top-down floor shots or extreme macros. The poster goes in as
Style + Composition + Lighting and is also sent to the image model; never Camera or Everything (the video copies
the poster's still frames and barely moves).

History: until 2026-10-28 the rule pushed energetic moves (crash zoom, crane, top-down, macro) against a static
camera. Those turned the storyboard's lived-in scenes into hand-led product demos; the owner preferred the
storyboard's own look (Oct 31 batch). See `references/rule-changes.md`.
