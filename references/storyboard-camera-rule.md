# Camera variety rule (proposed for the Storyboard skill; applied today at Ad brief level)

The public storyboard skill tends to write slow push-ins, slow slides and locked-off frames, so the videos come out
static. Until the skill itself is changed, the Ad brief fixes it (camera direction per scene row) and the poster is
used for Style + Composition + Lighting only. The rule, as it would go into the skill's shot design rules:

- Every panel names ONE clear camera move. Default to energetic moves: fast push-in, crash zoom, orbit (45-90
  degrees), crane up or down, top-down to eye level, low-angle dolly through a foreground object, handheld follow
  of the hand, parallax slide past a foreground object, whip pan into the next beat.
- Vary the angle across panels: at least one overhead or top-down, one low angle, one close macro, one wide.
  Never two panels in a row with the same angle and the same move.
- "Slow push-in", "slow lateral slide" and "locked-off" at most once each. Locked-off only on B04.
- A01 opens on movement: the hand action or the camera is already moving in frame one.
- Every move physically possible for a real camera; product geometry unchanged.
